The In-House Project

What we'll build with Standard Bank

The short answer

A dedicated Lawu Media creator unit, embedded with Standard Bank — producing platform-native video content from ideation to publish. We are physically there to shoot, we edit everything ourselves, and we deliver a consistent content cycle backed by real performance reporting.

01 — IDEATE

Video Ideation

Concepts engineered for the scroll-stop effect — built on trends, banking-culture insight, and what actually performs in South Africa.

02 — SHOOT

On-Site Production

Our crew shoots the content ourselves — at your branches, offices, and events. We handle gear, direction, and talent on the day.

03 — EDIT

In-House Editing

Every cut, caption, and sound choice is done by our own editors — fast turnarounds with full quality control, no outsourcing.

04 — SHIP & REPORT

Publish & Optimise

Consistent posting cycles with real-time analytics. We optimise cost-per-engagement and report in professional, bank-ready standards.

Why embedded matters: influencer posts often feel forced or "salesy" and damage brand trust. An in-house unit lives inside the brand — content stays authentic, on-message, and legally compliant, while moving at the speed social media demands.
